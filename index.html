Why

Music is not content.

The current system treats music like inventory. Infinite catalog, algorithmic playlists, fractions of a penny per stream. It works for major labels. It fails everyone else.

Scenes create culture. DJs, radio hosts, label heads, dedicated listeners — human networks that find, champion, and sustain the music that matters. Their work has value. They deserve to get paid for it.

We're not building another streaming platform. We're building the infrastructure that lets underground music communities fund themselves.

What we're building
Subcult
Product
Curated music communities with integrated streaming and direct payments. Record store, radio station, and venue — one place. Curators get paid for the work they already do.
Onda
Protocol
On-chain micropayment rails. Every play splits revenue between the artist, the curator who surfaced the track, and the community. No 30% platform cut.
MDRN
Network
Federated internet radio. Any community can run a station. Every station pays its curators and artists. Global distribution, local control.
